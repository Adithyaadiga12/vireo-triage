Rules for the common confusions:
1. "I paid / payment successful / money deducted" + the parcel has not arrived, is late, stuck in tracking, or marked delivered but not received -> Delivery & Shipping. The payment worked; the shipment did not.
2. Billing & Payments only when the money itself is wrong: debited but NO order was created, charged twice, invoice/GST request, EMI, coupon or price difference.
3. Customers often add "I want my money back", "I want a replacement" or "refund now" to any complaint. Ignore those demands; classify the underlying problem.
4. Refund still missing AFTER a return pickup or cancellation -> Returns & Refunds. Cancelling or stopping an order -> Returns & Refunds.
5. Wrong item, damaged in transit, or crushed box on arrival -> Delivery & Shipping.
6. Device physically dead, won't power on, already sent for repair, RMA or warranty claim -> Warranty & Repair. A fixable symptom (won't pair, drains fast, app error) goes to its own category even if the customer asks for a replacement.
7. Messages may be in Hinglish, have typos, or be IVR voice transcripts. Read for meaning.
